# CITY-06 and CITY-08: drivable garage vehicles and the company helicopter

Branch `claude/city-06-08-vehicles`, based on `origin/codex/empire-world` @ `60d7ace` (FREE-09 merged as PR #37). Author: Claude Opus 5.5 (Interface). Status: implemented, awaiting independent review; not pushed.

## Requirements (recorded before coding)

From the coordinator brief, AGENTS.md, COORDINATION.md, CITY_EXPANSION_PLAN.md (rows "Helicopter and aerial travel", step 5), TASKS.md (CITY-06, CITY-08), reports/free-09.md, DESIGN_STANDARD.md and the master Web Design Standard:

**CITY-06: bought vehicles are drivable like the executive sedan.**

- R1. Every bought garage vehicle (hatchback, van, coupe, SUV) can be entered at its driver's door, wherever it stands: in its bay, on its kerb slot or where it was last parked.
- R2. Entry and exit are animated like the sedan's: walk to the door, reach the handle, the door swings open, lower into the seat, the door closes, the engine starts (and the reverse on exit). The Kenney Car Kit models have no separate doors, so a door leaf matched to the body colour is added and hinged on the driver's side.
- R3. Driving uses the existing controls and drivetrain: arrow keys (or remapped keys), Shift to accelerate, the touch joystick, the interaction key to exit, the camera key for both views.
- R4. Each model has its own body footprint (from the pinned kit manifest × the kit's 1.8 scale), its own collider (height included) and its own seat, door, eye (first person) and chase-camera offsets.
- R5. Exiting parks the vehicle where you leave it. It stays there, solid, for the rest of the session. **Decision:** the parked position is not saved. On reload a bought vehicle returns to the slot its save records (its bay, or its kerb slot after "Bring out"). Reasons: world poses are not part of the authoritative empire save (the sedan also returns to its start on reload); the host and cloud coordinator cannot validate a free-form pose against client colliders, so a saved pose could put a car inside a building or another car on another device; a slot is always reachable and its deferred-collider rules (FREE-09 review 4) already hold.
- R6. Exiting is refused while moving, and when the driver's side is blocked the passenger side is tried, so you cannot be trapped in a car parked against a wall.
- R7. The garage dialog stops saying driving is coming later and explains how to drive (walk to the driver's door and press the interaction key) and that a car you leave elsewhere returns to its slot on reload. "Store in bay" is refused for a vehicle parked away from the garage (drive it back first) so a parked car never jumps across the city.

**CITY-08: the helicopter.**

- R8. A helipad on the **Meridian motor garage rooftop** (22 × 14 m flat roof at 6.3 m), reached physically by an external staircase on the garage's east wall. Ground-level space in Harbour works is taken by the plots, buildings and container yard; the rooftop keeps the pad next to the garage where vehicles are already managed.
- R9. **Decision: the helicopter is purchasable** with fictional city credits (45,000 cr) through the shared `applyCityAction` rules, with the per-click purchase nonce pattern (refusals: setup incomplete, already owned, insufficient credits, repeated nonce, invalid request). It is a capital ledger entry like other city purchases. It is recorded as an optional `helicopter` field in the `city` save block. Old saves (no `city` block, or a `city` block without `helicopter`) load unchanged without a helicopter. Before purchase the helicopter stands on the pad with a "for sale" sign; the pad kiosk opens the purchase dialog.
- R10. Board at the pilot's door (animated like the cars: approach, door, seat), rotor spin-up before lift-off, fly, land, exit only when landed on the pad or at street level (never on a building roof you could not climb down from).
- R11. Simple controls built on the existing actions so remapping keeps working: forward/back fly forward/back, left/right turn, Jump climbs, Shift (run) descends, the interaction key exits when landed. On touch screens the joystick flies and turns, and the Jump button becomes Up with a Down button beside it while flying.
- R12. Safety: a ceiling of 90 m above ground, a floor at the terrain or the top of whatever is beneath the airframe (you land on roofs and the pad, never fall through), the city bounds, and collision with building volumes (the airframe stops at walls and cannot descend into a building). You cannot get stuck: the only blocked directions are into solids, climbing is always possible below the ceiling, and exit is offered only where you can walk away.
- R13. Both camera views: a cockpit view, and a chase camera that pulls back and tilts down progressively with altitude so the skyline and the whole city come into view.
- R14. No suitable CC0 helicopter is in the pinned Kenney packs (Car Kit, City Kits), so the helicopter is a clean procedural model (fuselage, canopy, tail boom, rotors, skids) built from Three.js primitives in the city's palette. No new downloads.

## Design (as built)

- **Ride specs** (`src/client/rides.ts`): one record per drivable thing (sedan, four Kenney models, helicopter) with body half-extents, height, seat, door, handle, eye and chase distance. Kenney footprints come from the pinned manifest × 1.8, so a kit rebuild updates them. The sedan's numbers are its old hard-coded ones.
- **Player controller** (`player.ts`): `ride` selects the footprint for collision, bounds, the height for overhead clearance (never below the old 1.7 m) and the first-person eye. `flying` switches to `fly()`: horizontal collision as for cars, vertical between `airframeFloor` and `airframeTop` (`flight.ts`). The chase camera adds 0.6 m of distance per metre of altitude and tilts down to 0.78 rad, with a higher camera cap while flying.
- **Flight model** (`flight.ts`, `Rotorcraft`): rotor spool 2.2 s before lift, climb 6 m/s, descent 5 m/s easing to ≥1.2 m/s near the floor, cruise 22 m/s, reverse 6 m/s, yaw 1.1 rad/s, hover on release, no taxiing on the ground. Touchdown snaps the skids within 5 cm. `landingSpot` allows an exit only at street level or on the pad.
- **Traversal** (`traversal.ts`): any `Ride` is boarded and left through the sedan's existing phases (approach, reach, door, seat transition, foot IK, cues). Offsets come from the ride spec. The driver's side is tried first, then the passenger side (no door swing there). The sedan path and its unit test are unchanged.
- **World**:
  - Bought cars (`world/garage-rides.ts`, `districts.ts`) keep a persistent object per vehicle with a door leaf painted with the body's own palette cell, a dashboard, wheel and roof header, spinning/steering kit wheels and body lean.
  - Each has its own collider (one per bay, allocated once) that follows the vehicle and turns solid only once no live body or walker overlaps it (FREE-09 review 4 kept via `slotClear`, now given every live body with its own footprint).
  - In first person the opaque kit shell is hidden so the cabin framing and road show.
  - Bring out / Store are disabled for a vehicle parked away from the garage ("Drive it back first").
- **Helipad** on the garage roof (deck at 6.3 m, ring, H, edge lights, rails with a north-east opening, a 22-step staircase on the east wall, rising 0.286 m per step, and a heliport kiosk). The procedural helicopter (`world/helicopter.ts`, eleven draw calls) has a hinged glazed pilot door, cockpit, canopy, spinning main and tail rotors, and a static disc under reduced motion.
- **Economy**: `buy-helicopter` in `applyCityAction` (host, cloud intents, fixture), ledger label "Helicopter purchase · Company helicopter", purchase tracker target `helicopter`.
- **Touch**: while flying, Jump reads Up and a Down button holds the run action (the stick no longer sends run in flight). View moves aside.
- **Minimap**: G · H on the garage.

## Performance (FREE-09 method)

Headless Chrome on ANGLE/Metal, vsync off, Medium preset, same viewpoints plus a new `helipad` view. Before is `60d7ace` exported to a scratch folder with its own fixture (port 5022); after is this branch (port 5021, with a bought hatchback and the helicopter present). Run back to back. Mean frame time in ms (`city-06-08/perf-*.json`):

| View | Desktop 1440x900 | Phone 874x402 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founding street | 3.01 → 3.13 | 2.65 → 2.82 |
| Business street | 2.60 → 2.59 | 2.08 → 2.18 |
| Aerial | 3.08 → 3.04 | 2.70 → 2.87 |
| Helipad | 3.40 → 3.49 | 3.12 → 3.24 |

The worst phone regression is **+0.17 ms**, within the +1.5 ms budget, at about +30 draw calls. This was measured headless, not on a device. The after run predates the last commit's first-person shell toggle, which only changes visibility in the cockpit view.

## Deferred and not verified

- **Real devices.** No iPhone, Android or desktop-app runs. All evidence is headless Chrome with touch emulation.
- **Audio.** The helicopter reuses the engine loop at rotor rpm, with no tyre roll. Authored rotor audio is deferred.
- **Door animation on the passenger side.** There is none (no hinged leaf there). The Kenney door leaf is a flat panel in the body colour, shown only while open.
- **Collision volume.** The main rotor disc is not collided with; the fuselage and tail are.
- **Parked poses.** Poses are per session by decision (R5). A saved pose would need host-side validation.
- **No landing on other roofs.** Landing on other roofs is possible, but exiting there is refused by design.
