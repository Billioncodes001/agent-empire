# ORG-01 / ORG-02 report: executive roster and offices, estimates before work

Branch `claude/org-01-02` (worktree-isolated), based on `codex/empire-world` with LIFE-04 (`d2f6575`) merged in. Spec: [ORG_PLAN.md](../ORG_PLAN.md). Not pushed.

## What was built

### ORG-01: Executive roster and offices
- **`src/shared/org.ts`** has `OrgState`, `Executive` and `ExecutiveRole`, exactly as the plan specifies, plus stable names (`Alex · Chief of staff`, `Morgan · Operations`, `Priya · Finance`, `Jordan · Marketing & sales`, `Sam · Research`).
- **`src/server/org.ts` `projectOrg()`** is pure and unit-tested. All executives are `rule_based` and make no model calls.
  - Chief of staff wraps supervision. Its attention is plans awaiting approval.
  - Operations reports running, queued, blocked (`blockedBy`) and waiting workers (`waitingOn`), plus held dependency failures (`heldDependencyId`, which is its attention) and items held for budget.
  - Finance reports today's spend against the daily budget, approved job budgets against actual spend, and budget increases needed (its attention).
  - marketing_sales and research show "Not staffed yet".
- **Protocol:** `welcome.org` (optional, so older servers still work) and a new `org` server message. The server broadcasts the message only when the roster signature changes, never because a timestamp moved.
- **World:** the loft was re-planned around a row of five compact executive desks along the north glass.
  - Each desk has a brass name plate. Unstaffed desks read "NOT STAFFED YET".
  - The founder's desk moved toward the south wall, and the couch and plant were moved.
  - Seated bodies: the existing chief of staff Person moved to its desk, and operations and finance got their own. Each has a stable distinct suit, never the CEO's (`executiveOutfit`). Unstaffed roles have no body.
  - Executives sit on HQ floor 1 only. They are not `DESKS` and never use funded capacity.
  - Loft interactables carry a `y`, so the meeting area below doesn't pick them up.
- **E at an executive desk** shows a hint ("Name · Rule-based · N waiting for you"), then opens the executive card. The card shows the role, Rule-based label, live summary and attention, with a link to the founder desk or the queue until ORG-03 adds the inbox. Clicking or tapping a desk works the same way.
- **Management → Overview** has a new "Executives" section with five cards. Cloud mode says executives are not available there yet.
- **Clearance tests** (`tests/office-furnishing.test.ts`) check that:
  - loft furniture stays inside the loft;
  - the stair landing, a 1 m aisle and the posts stay clear;
  - each executive sits in its own chair, and the approach spots are clear;
  - executive desks are not workstations.

### ORG-02: Estimates before work
- **`src/server/estimate.ts` `estimateJob()`** is pure.
  - Each item gets a p25–p75 cost and wall-clock duration from finished `done` tasks with recorded spend. Same-phase history is used when there are at least 3 samples, otherwise all history.
  - With fewer than 3 samples it uses the stated default: $0.50–$3.00 and 10–45 min per item. The UI then says "Rough default — no history yet".
  - Calendar time uses LIFE-04's real `itemDependencies` (including `parallel`: a parallel item runs beside the previous one, and the next ordered item waits for both) and `maxWorkers`.
- **Durable per-task spend** is kept in `usage.json` (`Ledger.recordTaskCosts`, `taskCost`, `taskCosts`).
  - Records are keyed per task and worker, kept as a high-water mark, and summed over retries.
  - Recording never double-books the totals.
  - An old `usage.json` loads unchanged.
- **Consultancy changes:**
  - Each item has a `phase` (default build). The fingerprint only includes a non-build phase, so old plans keep their fingerprint.
  - A live `estimate` is shown while the job is pending and frozen at approval.
  - `approvedBudgetUsd` defaults to the top of the range rounded up. The CEO can edit it on the founder desk, through `POST …/approve {budgetUsd}`, or with `/approve ID 25` on Telegram.
  - Each job and item shows `actualSpendUsd`, labelled "provider-reported list cost".
  - An old `consultancy.json` loads unchanged and is not rewritten.
- **Over budget:** `reconcile()` records `budgetHold {reachedAt, spentUsd, approvedBudgetUsd}` on the job.
  - The queue's new `taskHold` hook keeps that job's unstarted items queued, unfailed. It sits beside LIFE-04's dependency gate, and running work is never killed.
  - Finance's `attention` counts the hold.
  - `raiseBudget()` exists for ORG-03 to release the hold.
- The **founder desk** job review shows cost, calendar time, workers, the basis, per-item phase and range, and an editable budget. After approval it shows the approved budget against actual spend, with a meter and a hold notice.
- Telegram `/job` shows the estimate text.

## Review fixes

The independent review verdict was ACCEPT WITH FIXES. Each fix has a test that failed before the fix, except fix 3 and the crowded-parallel estimate, which cover behaviour that was already correct but untested.

1. **The budget hold came too late** (`d9d84a3`). `holdReason()` read only the hold that `reconcile()` saves, and `reconcile()` runs on the 5-second timer. So when item N finished over budget, the queue could seat item N+1 first.
   - `Consultancy.activeHold()` now checks spend against the budget live, and both `holdReason()` and the job projection use it. The saved `budgetHold` stays as the record.
   - The server's `taskHold` hook records the latest worker spend (`ledger.recordTaskCosts`) before checking.
   - Test: approve with a $3 budget, finish item 1 at $3.20, let the queue pump without `reconcile()`. Item 2 stays queued and no second worker is hired.
2. **A held task kept its continuity desk reservation** (`d434230`). `seat()` now drops the reservation before skipping a held task.
   - Test in `life-04.test.ts`: reserve the dependency's desk while the send-home is still pending, then put the task on hold. The reservation is gone.
3. **No test for item order reaching the queue** (`c6b26d4`). In `org-server.test.ts`, with concurrency 0, approving a two-item plan gives queue task 2 `dependsOn = [task 1]`.
4. **Minor fixes** (`bbdefb0`):
   - A task's spend record is frozen once it has `finishedAt`, so the CEO chatting with a finished worker before it goes home neither counts toward the job nor triggers a hold.
   - `/approve ID 25 extra` is refused instead of the trailing words being ignored, and there is a test for `/approve ID 25`.
   - The founder desk's hold copy now says "until the budget is raised (coming with the approval inbox)".
   - New estimate test: more parallel items than `maxWorkers`.

5. **Freezing at `finishedAt` lost the final turn's cost.** The Stop hook finishes the task, and usage is re-read about 300 ms later, after the freeze had already happened.
   - A finished task's spend record now keeps growing while its worker stays `done`, `exited` or `offline`.
   - When the CEO gives that worker new work (status leaves done), `taskCostObservations` emits a `frozen` marker. From then on the record never grows. The marker is persisted in `usage.json`.
   - Ledger test: an observation at finish is followed by a higher cost from the rescan, and the higher cost is counted. After a reprompt, later growth is ignored even once the worker is done again. A retry by a new worker still adds to the task, and the freeze survives a restart.
6. **Costly work per queued task on every queue pass.**
   - Spend is now recorded once per seating pass through a new queue event, `beforeSeat`, which runs before any hold check.
   - `taskHold` is side-effect free and returns at once for tasks that are not client jobs.
   - Test: `beforeSeat` runs once per pass, not once per task, and `holdReason` never reads spend for non-client tasks.
