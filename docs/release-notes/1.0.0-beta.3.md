Agent Empire 1.0.0-beta.3: the city grows while you're away, and cloud mode can now plan and send email.

**The city architect**
- On a server install, your city keeps growing a little each day while you're offline: street lamps, trees, café parasols, awnings and more.
- It costs nothing with free rule-based growth or a local model. With your own API key it's capped (5 US cents a day and $1 a month by default).
- Every change is listed in your inbox. You can undo any change, revert a day, or pause it.
- Turn it on in Settings › Let the city grow while you're away.

**Cloud mode does more**
- The approval inbox, research and company memory now work without your office computer.
- The chief can plan in cloud mode with a free hosted model or your own API key. Spending is capped, and plans wait for your approval.
- Outreach email works in cloud mode with your own mail account, and you approve every message.

**Characters**
- New jump and driving animations from a free animation library.

**Fixes**
- The Android setup screens no longer run off the edge, on any screen size or text size.
- Setup remembers your progress if the app reloads or Android closes it in the background. Passwords and keys are never saved.
- Settings › Updates shows the exact game build, so you can tell versions apart.

**Installing**
- Mac: download `Agent-Empire-Mac-Apple-Silicon.dmg` (M1 or newer) or `Agent-Empire-Mac-Intel.dmg` (older Intel Macs), open it and drag Agent Empire to Applications. Not signed by Apple yet: the first time, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
- Windows: download and run `Agent-Empire-Windows-Setup.exe`. Not code-signed yet: if Windows warns you, choose More info › Run anyway.
- Android: download `Agent-Empire-Android.apk` on your phone and open it (allow installing from your browser when asked). The app tells you when an update is ready.
- iPhone and iPad: open your office address in Safari and choose Share › Add to Home Screen.

Installed apps update themselves: the game updates in place, and the app tells you when a new download is ready.
