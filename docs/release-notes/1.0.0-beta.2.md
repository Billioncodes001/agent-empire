Agent Empire 1.0.0-beta.2 makes the city feel more solid and the release simpler.

**Characters and controls**
- Characters can no longer stand on or walk into cars, and running looks right, with much less foot sliding.
- Phones and tablets get a Run button, and the Use button now says what it does: Enter, Board or Exit.
- Game controllers work in browsers that support them.
- Cars can't climb stairs, and the helicopter flies over them.

**Fixes from device testing**
- Installed beta apps now find their updates.
- A long press on a button no longer selects its text on Android.
- Android release builds say clearly when an address needs HTTPS.
- Onboarding messages no longer cover the dialog, and Tab stays inside dialogs in Safari.

**Simpler downloads**
- The release page now has a few clearly named files.
- Obtainium users: change your APK filter to `^Agent-Empire-Android\.apk$`.

**Installing**
- Mac: download `Agent-Empire-Mac-Apple-Silicon.dmg` (M1 or newer) or `Agent-Empire-Mac-Intel.dmg` (older Intel Macs), open it and drag Agent Empire to Applications. Not signed by Apple yet: the first time, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
- Windows: download and run `Agent-Empire-Windows-Setup.exe`. Not code-signed yet: if Windows warns you, choose More info › Run anyway.
- Android: download `Agent-Empire-Android.apk` on your phone and open it (allow installing from your browser when asked). The app tells you when an update is ready.
- iPhone and iPad: open your office address in Safari and choose Share › Add to Home Screen.

Installed apps update themselves: the game updates in place, and the app tells you when a new download is ready.
