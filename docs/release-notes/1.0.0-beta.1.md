The first public beta of Agent Empire: run an AI company as a city you can walk through. It is free to use with free, local tools, and you can link your own premium accounts when you want them.

**Free by default, premium when you want it**
- Free AI workers with Qwen Code and local models through Ollama. No account and no cost.
- Sign in to your own Claude account in Claude Code, or add an Anthropic or OpenAI API key, for frontier models.
- Free cloud mode: jobs run in GitHub Actions in your own repository, so your computer doesn't have to stay on.

**Your company**
- A chief of staff who plans client work, reviews results and proposes next steps. Everything waits for your approval.
- An approval inbox, company memory built from lessons you approve, and one GitHub repository per product.
- First-run setup for your company profile, workers, notifications (Telegram, ntfy or email) and approval rules.

**Voice and phone**
- Call your chief from the in-game phone. Speech runs offline on your office computer with free models you choose to download.

**The city**
- Two new districts (Meridian and Harbour works), plots you can buy and a garage.
- Drive the cars you buy, and buy a helicopter you fly from the garage roof. Everything is bought with fictional city credits, never real money.

**Installing**
- Android: install the APK below. The app tells you when an update is ready.
- iPhone and iPad: open your office address in Safari and choose Share › Add to Home Screen.
- Mac and Windows: these builds aren't code-signed yet. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose Open the first time; on Windows, choose More info › Run anyway.
